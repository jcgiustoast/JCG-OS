# Max Sturtevant (Max Wellcopy) Content Playbook

> Scraped 2026-11-25: last 90 days in detail plus all-time best. Numbers are from public pages; his hours are estimates.
> Copy his method, not his words. Everything in Juan's version uses Juan's voice and topics.

## 1. The picture in one paragraph

Max runs an email and SMS agency (Well Copy, ~90 people, 100+ brands on retainer). He posts about 50 times a week across five platforms. Most of it is cheap: a screenshot of an email plus a verdict ("this is a 0/10 order confirmation"). The raw material is free — his and his clients' inboxes fill with emails every day. A few posts a week are long and personal (life lessons, agency honesty, grind stories); those win reach and comments. The screenshot posts win saves. Nearly every post carries proof ("$500M+ for clients"). Every profile points to one paid community (Skool, $247/mo, ~438 members). Brands that follow him for 6–24 months book a call with the agency. It works because volume keeps his name in front of the same people daily, the formats repeat so each post is quick, and a scheduler, an auto-repost tool and keyword auto-DMs do the follow-up — he hardly ever replies by hand.

## 8. What not to copy

1. Volume watered down LinkedIn (median likes ~100 → ~60).
2. Zero replies — works at 138K followers, not when starting small.
3. His audience is his trade, not buyers. Juan's must be founders/heads of growth.
4. Off-niche virality is worthless (274K TikTok, no buyers).
5. Unlabelled sponsored posts — disclosure risk.
6. Generic hot takes get comments, not expertise.
7. Own case studies are his worst YouTube format; famous brands beat "my client".
8. Gen-Z slang and swearing are his voice, not Juan's.
9. Weekend daily posting added count, not results (except Sunday on X).
