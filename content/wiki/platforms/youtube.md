# YouTube

Channel: youtube.com/@JCGiusto

## What it's for

- Depth. A 20-minute video shows how Juan thinks better than any post can.
- Search. How-to videos keep getting found for years.
- Free guides. Each long video becomes a guide behind a keyword on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- Audience: DTC founders doing $2–50M a year.

## What works there (Max's evidence)

- Studies of famous brands are his breakout format. Median 12.9K views in the last year, his best ever at 187K.
- Tool how-tos are the steady base. People find them by search. He remakes the big ones every year.
- His own client case studies are his worst format. Viewers want famous brands, not "my client".
- His views jumped in 2026 when thumbnails moved from "my revenue" to "famous brands' revenue".
- Long videos bring 88% of his views. Shorts get about 2K views each.

Examples:
- Brand study: "I Studied Three $100M Brands. Their Strategies Contradict Each Other" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zjz_0zONuVI (187K)
- Brand study: "I Studied Every $100M Ecommerce Offer So You Don't Have To" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SjSkuT5BtTA (68K)
- How-to: "Klaviyo Email Marketing Tutorial For Beginners (FULL SETUP)" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=79_e8-lBbmo (137K)
- Became a free guide on LinkedIn: "The Most Valuable Ecommerce Funnel Training You'll Ever Watch" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KppxBkMCbkI (55K)

## Juan's formats here

| Format | What it covers | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Brand study | Checkouts, offers, product pages or subscription setups of famous public brands. Never men's health. | 14–25 min |
| Yearly how-to | Shopify and GA4 analytics, A/B test setup, checkout extensions, Claude for CRO | 10–30 min |
| Live page fix (later) | Fixing a public brand's product page on screen | 25–45 min |
| Free course (later) | Several how-tos stitched into one long CRO course | 1–3 h |

Shorts: the same vertical video as the Reel and the TikTok. Rankings and "pages worth stealing".

## Weekly volume and slots (ET / Madrid, all scheduled in advance)

1 long video a week from week 4. 3 Shorts a week (2 in week 2, 3 from week 3).

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | 11:00 / 17:00 Short (same video as the Reel) |
| Tue | 11:00 / 17:00 long video (from week 4) |
| Wed | 11:00 / 17:00 Short (same video) |
| Fri | 11:00 / 17:00 Short (same video) |

A second long video waits until hours allow or an editor is hired, which is not before the end of month one.

## How each video is made

- **Filming.** Juan at his desk. Screen recordings of real pages. No green screen.
- **Opening, under 60 seconds.** A puzzle question. The short answer. The brands in the video. One line on who Juan is. What the viewer will get. Then start. No "welcome back".
- **Body.** A numbered list. Split by brand size where it helps. Real pages on screen, never slides alone.
- **Ending.** Name the one next video and say why to watch it.
- **Chapters.** Add them. They help search, and Max mostly skipped them.
- **Titles.** Use these shapes:
  - "I studied every $100M brand's checkout so you don't have to"
  - "Shopify A/B Testing Tutorial 2027 (FULL SETUP)"
  - "Watch me fix this brand's product page (LIVE)"
  - "If you don't understand contribution margin, your tests are lying"
- **Thumbnails.** Brand studies: famous products with a revenue badge, Juan's face small or absent, few words. How-tos: Juan's face, 2–3 big words, the tool's logo. One Figma template. No designer before the end of month one.
- **Description.** One template on every video. Order: the jcgiusto.com signup page with the free guide, MentorPass, the other four accounts, two lines about Juan. Bio line: "Head of eCommerce at a $100M subscription brand."
- **Pinned comment.** Juan's own, with the free-guide link.
- **Shorts.** Verdict in the first three seconds. About a minute long. Title is the hook.

## How it gets fed by reuse

- One vertical video becomes the Reel, the TikTok and the Short. Film once, post three times.
- Each long video becomes a free guide. The checklist or notes behind the video go behind a keyword in the Friday LinkedIn post and the Friday Reel.
- Cut-downs of the strongest minute can become a Reel later.
- How-tos come back each year with the new year in the title.

## Platform limits

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| Long video length | 15 minutes until the account is verified by phone; then up to 12 hours |
| Short length | Up to 3 minutes (since October 2024); vertical or square |
| Short shape | 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920 |
| Long video shape | 16:9 |
| Title | 100 characters (about 40 show on Shorts tiles) |
| Description | 5,000 characters |
| Thumbnail | 1280 x 720, under 2MB |

Sources: https://www.socialync.io/blog/social-media-platform-limits-guide-2026 (updated November 2026), https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-character-limits/, https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be. Not checked against YouTube's own help pages. Verify the channel by phone before the first long video.

## What not to copy from Max

- Case studies of his own clients. They are his worst format, and Juan can't share client or work numbers anyway.
- Podcasts with guests. Low views. Mainly useful for relationships and sponsors.
- The tool sponsor in his videos. Juan does no paid tool deals.
- "Day in the life" Shorts. They are on the do-not-post list.
- Saying which tools Juan's employer uses. Pick tools any DTC brand uses.
- His slang. Juan's voice is direct and plain.

## Hard limits

- No Mars Men numbers, tests or implementations.
- No ASTEROI client names or numbers. ASTEROI is not named publicly.
- No men's-health, hormone, testosterone or men's-supplement brands in rankings or studies.
- No Meta ads, ad creative or ad-spend advice. A brand study covers the site, not the brand's ads.
- No paid tool sponsorships.
- Every video points to the email list. MentorPass is the only paid link.
