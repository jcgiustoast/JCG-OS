# Instagram

Account: instagram.com/jc.giusto (the only handle that is not "jcgiusto").

## What it's for

- Reach. Short ranking videos get seen by founders who don't follow Juan yet.
- Email signups. A comment with the word AUDIT triggers an automatic DM. The DM links to the signup page on jcgiusto.com. The signup page delivers the 40-point product page audit checklist.
- Audience: DTC founders doing $2–50M a year. Not other marketers, not shoppers.

## What works there (Max's evidence)

- Ranking videos win. Score 5–8 real examples of one thing, worst first. His best one passed 480K views and got 746 comments.
- "Steal this" videos and carousels earn saves. People keep what they can copy.
- Keyword posts get the most comments of anything. Each comment fires an automatic DM for months.
- Zero hashtags on 256 posts. Reach came from the format, not tags.
- Videos run about a minute (median 59s). Own voice, not trending audio.

## How each piece is made

- **Filming.** Juan at his desk, on camera. No green screen. The page being scored sits in the top half of the frame; Juan's face sits in the bottom half.
- **Hook.** The verdict comes in the first three seconds. Example: "This is a 2 out of 10 product page." Name the page type and the score. Give people something to copy or argue with.
- **Length.** 45–75 seconds. One page type per video.
- **On screen.** A big score on each example. Captions burned in, because most people watch muted.
- **Caption.** One line that repeats the hook. Then a fixed footer: what Juan does, and "Comment AUDIT for my 40-point product page checklist."
- **Hashtags.** None. Max used none. The platform allows at most five.
- **Cover.** The score and the page type in big type, so the grid reads as a series.
- **Carousels.** The post-style card in Juan's purple and grey. Juan approves the template before it is built. The cover makes one bold claim. Slide 2 says what is coming.
- **Bio.** "Head of eCommerce at a $100M subscription brand." Never Mars Men. Link goes to the jcgiusto.com signup page. MentorPass is the only paid link.
- **Highlights.** About Juan → Free guides → MentorPass. No "Results" highlight, because results would mean work numbers.
- **Comments.** The DM tool answers the keyword. Juan answers real questions by hand. A small account needs the replies.

## How it gets fed by reuse

- One vertical video becomes the Reel, the TikTok and the YouTube Short. Film once, post three times.
- Both carousels are LinkedIn carousels from the day before. Same design, square or tall version.
- A ranking that wins comes back a month or two later with new pages.

## Platform limits

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| Reel length | Up to 20 minutes (Juan's target: under 75 seconds) |
| Reel shape | 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920 |
| Carousel slides | Up to 20 |
| Carousel shape | 4:5 portrait (1080 x 1350) or 1:1 square |
| Caption | 2,200 characters, hashtags included |
| Hashtags | 5 per post (cut from 30 in December 2025) |
| Bio | 150 characters |
| Story | Up to 60 seconds per frame |

Sources: https://metricool.com/instagram-limits/ (January 2026), https://lettercounter.org/blog/instagram-reels-caption-length/ (hashtag cut), https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length (Reel length). Aspect ratios are standard practice. They were not checked against Instagram's own help pages.

## What not to copy from Max

- His volume. He posts about 13 times a week. Juan posts about 6 plus stories.
- Ignoring comments. That works at 138K followers, not at the start.
- Paid tool posts without a label. Juan does no sponsored posts at all.
- Off-topic hits. Views from people who will never run a DTC brand are worth nothing.
- His slang and swearing. Juan's voice is direct, not Gen-Z.
- Green screen. Juan films at his desk.
- "Day in the life" posts. They are on the do-not-post list.

## Hard limits

- No Mars Men numbers, tests or implementations.
- No ASTEROI client names or numbers. ASTEROI is not named publicly.
- No men's-health, hormone, testosterone or men's-supplement brands in rankings or studies.
- No Meta ads, ad creative or ad-spend advice.
- No paid tool sponsorships.
- Every post points to the email list. MentorPass is the only paid link.
