# TikTok

Account: tiktok.com/@jcgiusto

## What it's for

- Extra reach for almost no extra time. Every TikTok is the same file as an Instagram Reel.
- Not a place to build a separate show. If it stays small, it costs about 15 minutes a week.
- Audience: DTC founders doing $2–50M a year. Views from anyone else don't count.

## What works there (Max's evidence)

- Rankings win by far. Median 11.9K views, best 89K. Every other format sits near 1K.
- Shoppers argue with the scores in the comments. The arguments push the video further.
- Saves run at 4.6% of views. People keep rankings and "steal this" lists.
- His biggest hits ever (274K) were college-life videos. They brought no buyers.
- He went quiet for 19 months, then relaunched with rankings. The relaunch video got 89K views.

Examples:
- Ranking: "This is a 0 out of 10 order confirmation email…" — https://www.tiktok.com/@wellcopymax/video/7672412934127013134 (89K views, 3.5K saves)
- Ranking: "This is a 0 out of 10 pop up" — https://www.tiktok.com/@wellcopymax/video/7673883781463362829 (32K)
- Steal this: "10 out of 10 marketing emails I've received in 2026" — https://www.tiktok.com/@wellcopymax/video/7686287436825120013 (23.8K)

## Juan's formats here

| Format | What Juan scores or shows |
|---|---|
| Ranking, 0/10 to 10/10 | Product pages, checkouts, subscribe-and-save offers, cart drawers, quiz funnels |
| Best pages this week | Pages worth stealing, with why each one works |
| Ranking + free guide | The Friday ranking, ending with the AUDIT checklist |

Same three videos as Instagram. Nothing made only for TikTok.

## Weekly volume and slots (ET / Madrid, all scheduled in advance)

3 videos a week (2 in week 2, 3 from week 3).

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | 19:00 / 01:00 same video as Monday's Reel |
| Wed | 19:00 / 01:00 same video as Wednesday's Reel |
| Fri | 19:00 / 01:00 same video as Friday's Reel |

The 01:00 Madrid slot is never posted by hand. The scheduler does it.

## How each video works here

- **Filming.** Juan at his desk. No green screen. The page sits in the top half; Juan's face in the bottom half. This is the layout Max uses on TikTok.
- **Hook.** The verdict in the first three seconds. Example: "This is a 1 out of 10 subscription offer." Big public brands make strong targets, as long as they are not men's health.
- **Ask.** "Save this" or "send this to whoever runs your site". No "follow for more".
- **Length.** About a minute. One page type per video.
- **On screen.** A boxed title at the top, the score on each example, burned-in captions.
- **Caption.** Empty or 1–5 words. No hashtags. Max uses none.
- **Clean file.** Upload the file from the editor, not a download from Instagram. A watermark from another app can cut reach.
- **Bio.** "Head of eCommerce at a $100M subscription brand." Never Mars Men. The bio link goes to the jcgiusto.com signup page.
- **Comments.** No automatic DMs here. Point people to the link in bio. Answer founder questions by hand. Let shoppers argue on their own.

## How it gets fed by reuse

- One vertical video becomes the Reel, the TikTok and the YouTube Short. Film once, post three times.
- A ranking that wins comes back a month or two later with new pages.
- Lists from LinkedIn carousels can become a TikTok photo post later. Test it only once the three videos run smoothly.

## Platform limits

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| Video length | Up to 10 minutes (Juan's target: about 60 seconds) |
| Video shape | 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920 |
| Photo post slides | Up to 35 |
| Caption | 2,200 characters through a scheduler; up to 4,000 typed in the app |
| Bio | 80 characters |
| File size | 4GB |

Sources: https://www.socialync.io/blog/social-media-platform-limits-guide-2026 (updated November 2026), https://bundle.social/tools/tiktok-character-counter (caption limits). Not checked against TikTok's own help pages. The 80-character bio is from memory, not checked.

## What not to copy from Max

- Off-topic videos for views. His 274K-view college clips sold nothing.
- Silent meme videos with trending sounds. They got under 1K views.
- Ignoring comments. That works for a big account, not a new one.
- His slang and swearing. Juan's voice is direct and plain.
- "Day in the life" videos. They are on the do-not-post list.
- Green screen. Juan films at his desk.

## Hard limits

- No Mars Men numbers, tests or implementations.
- No ASTEROI client names or numbers. ASTEROI is not named publicly.
- No men's-health, hormone, testosterone or men's-supplement brands in rankings or studies.
- No Meta ads, ad creative or ad-spend advice.
- No paid tool sponsorships.
- Every video points to the email list. MentorPass is the only paid link.
