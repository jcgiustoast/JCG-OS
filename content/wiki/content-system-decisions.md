## Hard limits (from contract and firewall, not open to change)

- No Mars Men numbers, tests or implementations.
- No ASTEROI client names or numbers.
- No men's-health, hormone, testosterone or men's-supplement brands in rankings or studies.
- No Meta ads, creative or ad-spend advice.
- Juan writes his content; Claude corrects and adapts it, and whatever Claude adapts must sound like Juan.

## Left to Claude

How the system is built: file layout, template formats, how scheduling is wired, and the weekly review sheet. Tool choices must stay inside the $60 budget, and Juan sees each one before anything is paid for.
