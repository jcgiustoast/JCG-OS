# Content Types & Formats

> Format catalog for the five platforms plus the blog. Each format maps to one or more platforms in [[repurpose]]. Select format based on topic (framework vs hot take vs case study) and platform fit.

## Short Take

- **Platforms:** Twitter single, LinkedIn short post
- **When:** Hot takes, observations, single insights, Contrarian Premises
- **Length:** Under 280 chars (Twitter) or 500-800 chars (LinkedIn)
- **Hook type:** Contrarian Premise or Economics Reveal
- **Structure:** Hook + one-sentence proof. No narrative.

## Thread / Multi-Part Post

- **Platforms:** Twitter (3-12 tweets)
- **When:** Step-by-step framework breakdowns, listicles, Economics Reveals with multiple numbers
- **Hook type:** any, but thread-1 must stand alone
- **Structure:** Hook tweet → numbered steps or framework parts → prescription/framework name

## Narrative Post

- **Platforms:** LinkedIn standard or long (1,000-3,000 chars)
- **When:** Case studies, lessons from experience, framework + context
- **Hook type:** Specificity, Credential Anchor, or Framework-First
- **Structure:** Hook → Context → Problem → Reveal → Framework → Application → Question

## Long-Form Article

- **Platforms:** Blog (jcgiusto.com)
- **When:** Full framework pieces, deep Economics Reveals, evergreen references
- **Length:** 1,500-3,000 words
- **Hook type:** Framework-First title or Contrarian Premise title
- **Structure:** see [[blog]] — Title → Opening → Problem → Framework → Implementation → Conclusion

## Case Study (Anonymized)

- **Platforms:** LinkedIn long or Blog
- **When:** Proving frameworks work with real numbers
- **Hook type:** Specificity
- **Structure:** Scenario → Diagnostic → Intervention → Result (numbers) → Framework abstracted
- **Phase 1 constraint:** No Mars Men identifiers. Can reference "a 9-figure subscription brand" but not specific products, team, or proprietary data.

## Five-Platform Formats (added 2026-11-25)

Twelve formats taken from how Max Wellcopy works ([[max-wellcopy-playbook]]), rebuilt for Juan's topics. His method, Juan's voice. Draft hooks are starting points, not lines to copy. Which slot each one fills: the weekly calendar in [[content-strategy]].

**Check before drafting any of them:** no Mars Men numbers, tests or implementations · no ASTEROI client names or numbers · no men's-health, hormone, testosterone or men's-supplement brand anywhere in the piece · no Meta ads, creative or ad-spend advice.

### Ranking (0/10 to 10/10)
- **What it is:** Score 5–8 real examples of one thing, worst first. Product pages, checkouts, subscribe-and-save offers, cart drawers, quiz funnels.
- **Beats:** Name the thing being ranked → the worst one, with its score in the first 3 seconds → each next one, one reason per score → the 10/10 → "Agree?" or the guide keyword.
- **Draft hook:** "Ranking subscribe-and-save offers from 0/10 to 10/10."
- **Platforms:** Reel + TikTok + Short (one file), LinkedIn carousel.
- **Extra check:** Every brand on screen is a public brand outside men's health. Screenshots from Juan's own swipe folder.
- **Time:** About 1.5 hours a video once the swipes exist.

### Pages worth stealing (10/10 to steal)
- **What it is:** The best product pages, landing pages or upsells Juan saw this week, framed as things to copy.
- **Beats:** Promise ("worth stealing") → one example per slide or per shot → what exactly to steal from each → save prompt.
- **Draft hook:** "10/10 product pages I'd steal in 2026."
- **Platforms:** X image, Instagram carousel, TikTok.
- **Extra check:** Same brand screen as rankings.
- **Time:** 30–45 minutes once the swipes exist.

### Checklist (non-negotiables)
- **What it is:** A short list of things every DTC brand must have in one area. Product page, subscription checkout, A/B test hygiene.
- **Beats:** Title "[Area] non-negotiables" → 6–10 items, one line each → one line on why the list matters.
- **Draft hook:** "Subscription checkout non-negotiables:"
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn carousel, Instagram carousel, X (text list or images).
- **Extra check:** Nothing that describes how Juan's employer does it.
- **Time:** About 1 hour including the carousel.

### Brand study
- **What it is:** "I studied every $100M DTC brand's checkout so you don't have to." Famous public brands only, outside men's health.
- **Beats:** Tension title → which brands and why → 3–5 patterns, each with screenshots → where the brands disagree → what a $2–50M brand should copy → guide plug.
- **Draft hook:** "I studied every $100M DTC brand's checkout so you don't have to."
- **Platforms:** YouTube long, then 3–5 cut-downs for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, then a LinkedIn resource post.
- **Extra check:** Brand list screened before research starts, not after.
- **Time:** 12–18 hours (research, script, filming, editing, thumbnail).

### Yearly tutorial
- **What it is:** A full setup walkthrough, redone each year. GA4 or Shopify analytics, A/B testing tool setup, checkout extensions, Claude for CRO.
- **Beats:** Year + "full setup" in the title → what you'll have at the end → step-by-step screen recording → common mistakes → guide plug.
- **Draft hook:** "Shopify A/B Testing Tutorial 2027 (Full Setup)."
- **Platforms:** YouTube long.
- **Extra check:** Use tools any DTC brand uses. Never say which tools Juan's employer uses.
- **Time:** 10–15 hours.

### Free course (mega-class)
- **What it is:** Several tutorials stitched into one long free CRO course. Only once 4+ tutorials exist.
- **Beats:** Course title → chapters → each chapter is an existing tutorial.
- **Platforms:** YouTube long, then a LinkedIn resource post.
- **Time:** 3–4 hours of stitching, once the tutorials exist.

### Life-lesson list
- **What it is:** A short list of lessons from Juan's path: Argentina → Madrid → running ecommerce for a US brand across time zones.
- **Beats:** Hook → 5–10 one-line lessons → one closing line.
- **Draft hook:** "What 10 years of ecommerce taught me about…"
- **Platforms:** X (Sunday slot), LinkedIn.
- **Extra check:** Lessons only. No numbers or events from work.
- **Time:** 30 minutes.

### Learning diary
- **What it is:** The safe version of Max's agency diary. What Juan learned or got wrong, in his own words. No work numbers, no client numbers.
- **Beats:** Admission → what he believed → what changed his mind → what he does now.
- **Draft hook:** "What I got wrong about subscriptions this year:"
- **Platforms:** X, LinkedIn.
- **Extra check:** If the lesson only makes sense with a work number, drop it.
- **Time:** 30–45 minutes.

### Resource (comment keyword)
- **What it is:** A post that gives away one of Juan's free resources to anyone who comments a keyword. First resource: the eCom Subscription Checklist (50+ tasks, a Notion list), keyword CHECKLIST.
- **Beats:** Why the resource exists → 3–5 things in it (a preview, not a tease) → "Comment CHECKLIST and I'll send it."
- **Draft hook:** "Comment CHECKLIST and I'll send my subscription eCommerce checklist."
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn (every other Friday), Instagram (automatic DM), X.
- **Extra check:** The link goes to the resource's jcgiusto.com page (name + email form), never straight to the Notion list. Current page: jcgiusto.com/the-ultimate-subscription-ecommerce-checklist/
- **Time:** 30 minutes per post (the resource already exists).

### Contrarian take
- **What it is:** One belief the audience holds that is wrong, with the math. Comes from the vault (the ROAS draft is one).
- **Beats:** The wrong belief → the math → what to do instead.
- **Draft hook:** "Your 3.0 ROAS is a 1.33."
- **Platforms:** X, LinkedIn.
- **Extra check:** Only inside Juan's topics. A hot take outside them gets comments, not buyers. Measurement takes stay off Meta ads tactics.
- **Time:** 20–40 minutes.

### Quote reaction
- **What it is:** Quote a post from a big DTC account and add one line plus the reason.
- **Beats:** Quote → one line → one reason.
- **Platforms:** X (Tuesday and Thursday slots, posted by hand).
- **Extra check:** Don't quote men's-health brand accounts.
- **Time:** 10 minutes.

### Live brand fix
- **What it is:** Juan fixes a public brand's product page on screen. Only after week 4, and only if hours allow.
- **Beats:** The page as it is → 3–5 fixes, each with the reason → before and after.
- **Draft hook:** "Watch me fix this brand's product page (live)."
- **Platforms:** YouTube (Thursday, later).
- **Extra check:** Public brands only, outside men's health. Never a client.
- **Time:** 4–6 hours.

## Platform Limits (check before any piece is marked Ready)

Checked on the platforms' own help and developer pages, 2026-11-25. Posts go out through the scheduler, so where the scheduler's limit is tighter than the app's, the scheduler's limit is the one that counts.

| Platform | Text | Images / slides | Video | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X (+ Threads) | 280 characters | 4 per post | 2 min 20 s | – |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | PDF up to 300 pages, 100MB | 15 min | 1:2.4 to 2.4:1 |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters, 5 hashtags | Carousel up to 20 | Reel up to 15 min through the scheduler; over 3 min isn't shown to new people | Through the scheduler: 4:5 to 1.91:1. Reel 9:16 |
| TikTok | 2,200 characters on videos, 4,000 on photo posts (through the scheduler) | Photo post up to 35 | 10 min through the scheduler | 9:16 |
| YouTube | Title 100, description 5,000 characters | – | Short up to 3 min. Long: 15 min until the channel is phone-verified | Short 9:16, long 16:9 |

Juan's own targets sit well inside these: vertical video about 60 seconds, carousels 6–10 slides at 4:5. X takes only 4 images, so a carousel goes to X as the cover plus 3 slides.
