# Weekly content v2

## Why

The calendar of 2026-11-25 needs about 20 separate ideas a week; only copies (Threads, Instagram carousels, the same video on three platforms) are reused. Juan wants the opposite: a short list of things only he can make, and Claude turning each into the right post for each channel. He asked that it follow how Max Wellcopy actually works.

## What Max does (checked against the Nov 2026 scrape, `content/raw/research/max-wellcopy-2026-11/`)

- Reuse is sideways, not long-to-short. Shorts are filmed separately (2 of 141 share text with a long video). One ranking video goes to Reel + TikTok (Shorts sometimes). X images get copied to Instagram weeks later; Instagram carousels are styled as X posts. Old X posts auto-repost ~2.2/day (median 168 days old).
- Few long posts: ~9 long LinkedIn posts in 90 days (~0.7/week), each with a photo of him. They earn the most likes and comments.
- Volume = cheap pieces: ~10 single-image screenshots a week on X (an email + a one-line verdict, framed "10/10, steal this"); lists typed in his notes app and screenshotted (his subject-lines post: 350 bookmarks); carousels (~3/week on LinkedIn).
- Not in his data: designed infographics, Threads, daily replies (he spends ~20 min a week), X versions of LinkedIn posts. He has a ~90-person team.

## Approaches considered

| Approach | Trade-off | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the 25 Nov calendar | ~20 separate ideas a week from Juan | Rejected: too much creation |
| Few long pieces, cut into everything (one long video → 3 Shorts) | Fewest ideas | Rejected: Max doesn't do it; his Shorts get ~2K views |
| **Max's way: a few cheap repeatable pieces, each reused sideways** | Volume comes from 10-minute pieces | **Chosen by Juan, 2026-11-26** |

## Decisions (Juan, 2026-11-26)

- Max's way, as above.
- Screenshots = (a) a real brand's page (product page, subscribe box, checkout, cart drawer, upsell) + Juan's 1–2 line verdict; Claude finds the pages in Chrome and takes the screenshots; (b) a list Juan types in his phone's notes and screenshots. Never client or Mars Men numbers.
- Framework carousels stay, weekly, starting from the six framework ideas already in Notion (growth formula, metric tree, 11 profitability levers, 7 habits, ICE, LTV).
- The long post's image matches the post: a photo of Juan for personal stories, an infographic for idea posts. Each framework carousel also becomes one summary infographic.
- Kept from before, not Max's: weekends off (Sunday X post + daily story only), 15 min/day of X replies, Threads copy of every X post.

## How Claude adapts each piece

- Long post → LinkedIn Tue (+ photo or infographic) → one long single X post Wed (no threads, like Max).
- Brand verdict (×5) → X image same day → Instagram story → best one LinkedIn image (Fri, non-Resource weeks) → all five into Wednesday's carousel → Instagram Thu → X images Wed.
- Notes list (×3) → X image → one expanded into next Monday's checklist carousel → Instagram Tue → X images Fri.
- Framework (×1) → Thursday carousel (Figma, /figma-post) → one summary infographic → Instagram Fri + X Mon.
- Video (×4) → same file to Reel, TikTok, Short, X; caption per platform.
- Quote reactions → X only.
- From Notion ideas → Claude drafts Monday's contrarian take and the Sunday life-lesson list; Juan approves.
- Free copies → Threads (every X post); X images to Instagram weeks later; old X winners auto-repost.
