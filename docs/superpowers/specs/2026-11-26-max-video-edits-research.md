# How Max edits his videos

Studied 2026-11-26 with Juan's OK. Videos downloaded to `content/raw/research/max-wellcopy-2026-11/videos/` (gitignored).

## Short videos (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

Files: IG Db3PqznPy6B (= TikTok 7672412934127013134, same file posted twice), IG DcBc-hZS1Vr, IG DdOyjXIRDrf, TikTok 7701703762968186125, YT Shorts Wi7TmhMsiAA, j3pLNKG6KEA. All 720x1280, 30 fps, 17–62 s.

### Ranking Reel (his winning format) — one strict template
1. **Shot:** one static front-camera selfie at home, chest-up, eyes 35–40% from top, face slightly right. Never re-framed, no zoom, no green screen.
2. **Screenshot:** tall emails in a left column (x 6–45%, y 5–96%), overlapping his face. Wide items (pop-ups) in the upper half, ~45–50% wide, 2–3 side by side. No border, shadow, rounding, zoom or animation. Personal data hidden with plain black boxes.
3. **Score badge:** "N/10", white medium sans on solid black rounded box, 16–20% of frame width, ~6% tall, top-right of the screenshot (16–29% from top). Appears on the cut with no animation.
4. **Captions:** platform-style, white regular sans, soft shadow, no box, ~3.8% of width (≈41 px at 1080), narrow block ~45% wide at 82–88% from top, centred under the face (~65% x). Lowercase, digits as numbers, 5–13 words per phrase, 2–3 s each, no word highlight, no emoji.
5. **Cuts:** a jump cut at each item only (1.2–1.7 per 10 s). After each cut ~0.2 s of face (finger count), then screenshot + badge hard-cut in. Every pause over ~0.3 s removed. 4.1–4.8 words/s.
6. **Order:** worst to best, 6–7 items, ~7 s each; 10/10 longest (9–13 s).
7. **Hook:** score on the first word at 0.0 s; worst example + "0/10" on screen by 0.25–0.45 s. No title card.
8. **Ending:** one-line takeaway (or tool mention), then stop. No end card, no "comment X".
9. **Audio:** dry voice only, −14 to −16 LUFS. No music, no sound effects.

### Other shorts
- **Transparent Background short (Max's "hack"):** 3 s title box at start (white Title Case on black, 3 lines, ~30% from top), cut-out body over full-screen screenshot, overlay cards change ~every 3 s, captions centred.
- **Podcast clip (editor-made):** letterboxed 16:9, word-by-word text with neon-green bold italic key words, red "BAD" sticker, red underlines.
- **Music-only "you be the judge":** b-roll + thin lowercase text, emails as full-height strips ~2.5 s each, closing "which was ur favorite?".

## Long YouTube videos

Files: Zjz_0zONuVI (3 × $100M brands, 16:13), SjSkuT5BtTA ($100M offers, 22:27), pjkqcWoN0NA (Klaviyo flows tutorial, 46:44). 1280x720.

1. **Cold open** on his face, no logo or title card. Puzzle within 0–6 s over a diagram of brand cards + a ticking dollar figure.
2. **First 60 s:** puzzle → stakes → credential (logo wall + green "N+ BRANDS" counter) → promise of exactly 3 things → "Later in the video…" (4–7 s of blurred previews) → "let's get into it". Content starts at 38–39 s.
3. **Pacing:** hook shots 0.6–3 s; body 11–14 s per shot; tutorial body 30 s+. No pause ≥0.5 s anywhere. 225–250 wpm.
4. **Face jump cuts** every 2–4 s, alternating 100% and 110–125% framing.
5. **Face cam over screen:** rectangle (22% w × 47% h) or circle (~38% of height), bottom corner, 2% margin, moves to the empty corner. ~50% of a brand study, ~92% of a tutorial.
6. **Screen recordings** always moving: smooth zoom/pan to the part being discussed, cursor visible, key line highlighted.
7. **Graphics language:** off-white #EEE canvas, green pills (#3DDC6A-ish) with black heavy grotesk, cream dollar pills with thin black outline, charcoal lesson cards with big white bold sans. Red boxes/arrows only to critique a page.
8. **Animation set:** dollar counters ticking up, cards/logos popping in one by one, lines drawing in, blur-in words, section labels blurred until their section (a recurring "map" diagram, returns 3×).
9. **No running captions** (at most 1 s of word-by-word caps at the start).
10. **Proof b-roll:** homepage screenshots framed with the brand wordmark under them, timed to each brand name; founder tweets; physical product as prop.
11. **Plugs:** agency pitch at ~37–40% of runtime, community at ~68–75%, slipped in at a section change.
12. **Ending:** recap → call to action → face for last 20 s (room for end-screen buttons) → names one next video + why → "thanks for watching" → hard cut.
13. **Music:** none or a very low bed (medium confidence); tutorials have music under the intro only.
14. **Brand study vs how-to:** studies ~50% face, dense graphics, recurring map + lesson cards, puzzle hook. How-tos >90% screen with circle cam, title card + numbered agenda, credentials-first hook, few graphics after minute one.

## What makes a moment work as a short (from the long videos)
Starts with a bold claim or puzzle → exactly one concrete example with a number (before/after, or two brands side by side) → closes on a rule ("do this if…"). Needs no "as I showed earlier". 30–90 s. Often already has a lesson card, counter or checklist that carries over to vertical. Avoid stretches that depend on scrolling software.
