## What he likes so far

- **Cover:** when the headline has a number, the purple cover with the number big in Archimoto and one phrase in Verde, filling the slide (picked over the dark text-only cover and a dark cover with a chart).
- **Point slide:** numbered header, one or two short paragraphs at Archivo 40, then a visual (chart, picture or big number) that fills the lower half down to the footer. A big number goes in a light Lila box (34%) filling that room, number and line in black (picked over a white card and a free-floating Lila number).
- **Chart slide:** when the point is a sum, show the math row by row — a horizontal waterfall, black cost bars stepping down from a Lila total, the result in Verde (picked over two bars and a single big number).
- **Insight slide:** dark (stars). When the key idea is a formula, split its terms into white cards instead of one quote box (picked over his quote box and a grey version).
- **Call to action:** his measured purple slide, unchanged (picked over dark and grey versions).
- **Closing:** purple, text at 100 and the reactions strip as wide as the text (picked over his 80 version and a dark one).
- **Single image:** the two-column comparison (Lila amounts, Verde conclusion), picked over a big stat in the Lila box and a solid purple quote card — filled, with each column in a white card from heading to amount (not left open on grey).
- **X:** keep the LinkedIn stack (text on top, visual filling the bottom) and widen it; don't split a point slide into left/right columns.
- **Infographic layouts:** a full five-card yes/no tree on the taller 1000×1500 frame, picked over a mind map and a short three-card tree.
- **Infographic:** grey background; white cards with a thin Lila top edge and black headings; Lila numerals; the title's second line on a Verde highlight; a white hub circle with a Lila outline; his usual avatar footer bottom-left. Purple only as an accent.
- **Density:** never 2–3 paragraphs of small text or packed cards; never a big empty band either. The content fills the space between title and footer.
- **How he picks (taste run, 2026-10-26 → 28):** fast, without reasons. He took the recommended version in 9 of 11 picks. He chose against it twice: the Lila box over a white card for a big number, and the two-column comparison over a big stat. Both times he went for the plainer side-by-side or boxed look over the one that matched an earlier pick. When a recommendation leans only on "it matches what you picked before", don't trust it much.
