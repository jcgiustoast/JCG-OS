# Hone / Facebath — Client Deal

## What it is
New DTC **skincare** brand, brand name **Facebath**, run by **Saad** (CEO). Side collaboration: **Juan + Martin (Tincho)**, plus a designer + dev sourced by Hone (or quoted by us as an add-on). Positioned to Saad as "Juan + Martin on the side," not an agency — he knows Juan is FT at Mars Men. AI engine and IP stay invisible; Saad only ever sees finished deliverables.

## Brand state (per Saad, 2026-06-03)
- **Launch target:** late July / first week of August 2026.
- **Brand:** logo done, packaging in final stages, voice mostly locked.
- **SKUs (4):** Facebath (hero) $80–120, Cleanser $30, Serum $40, Moisturizer $30.
- **AI LP ask:** "fast creation of multiple iterations."
- **Post-launch:** mostly CRO + LP creation.
- **Budget:** told us to "propose both" (fixed + retainer); gave no ceiling.
- **Paid acquisition:** probably hiring an FT media buyer (consumes our LPs).

## Proposal sent (2026-06-03)
**Launch — ~$25K** (our layer; **design + dev NOT included** — Hone sources, or we quote separately)
- Store strategy + build direction (architecture, offers, bundles, subscription)
- Subscription MVP: customer portal, cancel flow w/ deflection, dunning, compliance (click-to-cancel + auto-renewal terms), data sync. Subscriber notification emails = recommendations only (build needs copy + design).
- Conversion copy: homepage + 4 PDPs + supporting pages (~14 pages total incl. LPs)
- 3 launch landing pages, each an angle
- Analytics live day one + sales/ad-spend/margin dashboard
- We brief + QA the build

**Retainer — starts at launch** (design/dev also not included)
- CRO: **3–5 experiments/mo** + testing roadmap + biweekly session — **$6K/mo**
- Landing pages, copy included — **$1K each**
- ~4 LPs/mo ≈ **$10K/mo**, 8/mo ≈ **$14K/mo**. 12-month lock = discounted rate.
- Deferred subscription retention work (cancel-flow A/B, dunning tuning, portal AOV) lives here as CRO fuel.

**Terms:** launch 50% to start / 50% at go-live. Retainer net 30.
**Need from Hone to start:** brand voice guide + approved claims per product.
**Optional add-on:** email foundation (welcome + abandoned-cart flows) ≈ $3K+ (needs copy + design).

## Internal pricing (NOT shared with Saad)
- **Juan rate $200/hr** — ~1.7× his $120/hr floor ($250K salary ÷ 2,080h). Charge rate, not cost; hours are the internal floor, price is value.
- **Martin rate $67/hr** — 1/3 of Juan's charge rate.
- **LPs $1K each** — cost ~$120–150 via engine + human edit, ~85% margin. Below market ($1–2.5K custom).
- **CRO $6K/mo** — anchored on Juan's real rate of $5K for 4 experiments, strategy-only.
- **Launch built bottom-up:** Juan ~65h, Martin ~87h, 3 engine LPs, +15% scope-creep buffer ≈ $25K. Martin is ~7h over his 80h (2h/day) cap → a Klaviyo/subscription contractor absorbs it, or he runs slightly over.
- **Designer/dev add-on if we source:** ~$67K at 1.75× their cost (parked, not detailed).

## Scope-creep firewall
**OUT of launch (separate quote each):** photography/video/UGC, brand identity/logo/packaging, full email/SMS program, loyalty/referral/quiz, blog/SEO, international/multi-currency, store migration, paid media management, legal certification of claims, SKUs beyond 4, LPs beyond 3.
**Rules:** revisions capped at 2 rounds/deliverable (then $200/hr); anything off the IN list = written change order before work; Hone asset delays move the launch date, not the fee.
