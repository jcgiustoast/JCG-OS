# Mars Men Phantom Equity Plan — Analysis & Decision Record

## Status
**RESTATED AWARD RECEIVED (2026-09-28) — recommended to sign; Juan's signature pending.** Mars Health amended and restated the whole Plan effective **2026-09-24** and reissued Juan's award as a "Restated Award." Ben Smith (CEO) signed for MM on 2026-09-25. MM's framing: "same award value and the same start date for vesting, but with friendlier terms around change of control and any termination." **Verified against the documents — that's accurate.** Signing replaces the August agreement in full.

**Net read:** a real upgrade. It removes the single worst clause in the August version (the 6-month post-sale service rule) and turns the award into something Juan keeps after leaving. The trade-off is a new leash: Cause-firing or a covenant breach wipes everything, vested included.

### Earlier history (August 2026)
Juan signed the original award **as-is on 2026-08-01**. Before signing he asked for three changes — acceleration on a sale, a without-cause carve-out on the 6-month tail, and anti-dilution. **MM declined all three** (to keep the plan consistent across recipients), offering only non-binding "may consider case-by-case waivers" language. Juan signed anyway on the **ASTEROI-bridge** logic (salary + credential + cover, not an equity play), after logging a real let-down about trading down from PAK's better equity terms.
- **Acceleration ask:** declined then; **still Board-discretionary** in the restated version (unvested shares are wiped at a sale unless the Board chooses otherwise).
- **Without-cause carve-out ask:** declined then; **effectively granted and exceeded** by the restatement — the 6-month tail is gone entirely, so being cut after a sale no longer costs anything already vested.
- **Anti-dilution ask:** declined then; still no anti-dilution (share count fixed, percentage dilutes). The restated Plan does add that MM can't cut the share count or change the payout formula without Juan's written consent.

- **Delayed sale money doesn't count:** earnouts, escrow, holdbacks and later price adjustments are excluded from the payout base. Upside: Juan never has to pay anything back.
- **Money tied to side deals is excluded:** anything paid to stockholders for non-competes, consulting, or employment is carved out of Net Proceeds.
- **Board can adjust value** up or down if MM pays dividends, raises capital, or takes on unusual debt, to keep the award measuring "appreciation."
- **Large-payout tax rule (280G):** MM may require stockholder approval before paying; if refused, no payment. Unlikely to bite at Juan's size.

## What it is
Phantom equity = a **contingent cash bonus dressed as equity.** No shares, no ownership, no voting, no dividends. It pays a cash amount **only if Mars Health is sold** (a "Change in Control"). If the company never sells, it is worth **$0**.

## The grant
- **Participant:** **Juan Giusto Cruz** (individual), with the award issued to **ASTEROI LLC** (WY, 100% Juan) as his designee. Signed for MM by **Benjamin Smith, CEO**.
- **Phantom shares:** **11,175**
- **Strike price:** **$0**
- **Grant date:** originally July 20, 2026; restated award dated **September 24, 2026**
- **Vesting Commencement Date (VCD):** **October 20, 2025** (backdated ~9 months — favorable; unchanged)
- **Total plan pool:** 558,773 phantom shares. Juan's grant = exactly **2.0% of the pool.**
- **Governing law:** Delaware (Juan's MM contract is Wisconsin — different jurisdiction).

## What 11,175 is worth (load-bearing inference)
The share count was sized off the old **0.1%** (Juan confirmed). Math is consistent: 11,175 ÷ 558,773 = 2.0% of the pool; if the pool is 5.0% of fully-diluted, Juan's slice = **~0.1% of fully-diluted**, implying a fully-diluted count near ~11.175M. **CONFIRM against the actual cap table** — the whole model scales linearly with this fraction.

Crucial: it's **0.1% of Net Proceeds, not 0.1% of the headline price.** Net Proceeds = sale price − fees − the **full preferred liquidation stack** (original issue price + accrued Series A dividends + a **5% investor top-up**) − delayed/contingent payments. Phantom sits **behind** preferred, like common stock.

## The gates (all must be true, or $0)
1. A qualifying **sale** happens (>50% third-party acquisition, or substantially-all-assets sale). IPO, SPAC, financings, and liquidations don't count.
2. Some shares are **vested** — at the sale date, or at the date Juan left if earlier. Nothing vests before Oct 20, 2026.
3. **Net Proceeds exceed the preferred stack** (subordination).
4. Juan was **not fired for Cause** and **hasn't materially breached** his non-compete/confidentiality obligations before the sale.
5. Juan signs a **broad general release** within 60 days of closing.

(The August version's gate "still engaged 6 months after closing" is **gone**.)

## Vesting
- **Cliff, then linear.** Nothing vests before **Oct 20, 2026**. On that date, **12/36 (33%)** vests at once (credit for the 12 months since VCD), then **+1/36 per month to 100% at Oct 20, 2028.**
- Effective % = 0.1% × (full months since Oct 20 2025 ÷ 36), capped at 100%, zero before Oct 2026.
- **Vesting stops at whichever comes first: Juan leaving, or the sale closing.** What's vested at that point is what can pay.
- Cliff is **three weeks away** (as of 2026-09-28). After Oct 20, 2026, leaving MM no longer zeroes the award.

## Risks in the restated version

### The new leash — forfeiture of vested shares
- **Fired for Cause → everything forfeits, vested included.** Cause includes fraud, confidentiality leaks, gross negligence, felony — but also broader items: refusing duties, **"any deviation from any of the policies or directives of the Company,"** or a material breach of any agreement with MM. For those three softer items MM must give written notice and **10 days to fix it**. The Board can also decide *after* Juan leaves that Cause grounds existed.
- **Breach of restrictive covenants → everything forfeits, any time before a sale, even after Juan has left.** This ties the vested shares to his contract's covenants: **24-month post-term US non-compete/non-solicit** and **confidentiality for the term + 2 years** (see [[professional]]). Practical meaning: after leaving, ASTEROI taking on a competing men's-health brand could cost the vested award. The risk axis is product/demographic overlap, same as the [[professional]] non-compete rule. The Board makes the breach determination (with 10 days to cure if curable).

### Carried over from the August version
- **IPO does NOT trigger** — now explicit (IPO, direct listing, SPAC all excluded). Narrow technical carve-out: for restated awards, those exclusions don't apply to an event occurring before Sept 24, 2027 (or Juan's leaving date, if earlier) — it would still need to be a >50% acquisition, so rarely matters.
- **Only the FIRST qualifying sale counts** — a modest majority recap "uses up" the one trigger; a bigger later exit pays nothing extra. An *existing* investor buying to >50% is carved out as a non-event.
- **Board (Administrator) has sole discretion** on Net Proceeds, the denominator, non-cash valuation, and Cause; interpretations are "final, binding, conclusive," no de novo review, upheld absent "manifest fraud or bad faith."
- **Denominator dilutes over time** — measured at closing, it grows with every future raise/option/phantom grant; Juan's fixed 11,175 only shrinks as a %, with no anti-dilution. Future rounds hit twice: bigger denominator **and** a taller preferred stack ahead of him.
- **Unvested shares vanish at a sale** unless the Board chooses to vest them. No automatic acceleration.
- **Tax:** ordinary income, withheld, 409A; paid to a WY LLC while Juan is a **Spanish tax resident** → cross-border complexity. The restated agreement adds that Juan **represents he has taken tax advice**, will report and pay everything, and **indemnifies MM** for any tax cost from paying the LLC. Get an advisor before any payout.
- **LLC ownership covenant:** Juan must keep ASTEROI single-member and 100% his while it holds the award; any ownership change counts as a transfer (and transfers are void). Tincho's 60/40 is a profit share, not equity — fine today; **giving Tincho real equity would breach this.** See [[asteroi-ownership]].

## The replacement problem (historical — why the August signing wasn't free)
The phantom **replaced**, not added to, the contractual 0.1% in Juan's MM contract. The August Award Agreement's "Entire Agreement / supersedes prior agreements" clause extinguished the old right when Juan signed on 2026-08-01. Trade as it stood then:

| | Old (contractual 0.1%) | Phantom (Aug 2026) | Phantom (restated Sept 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forfeiture | Paid if there at the sale (likely) | Forfeit for ANY reason unless engaged 6mo post-close | Vested survives leaving; forfeit only for Cause or covenant breach |
| Cliff | Likely none | $0 if sold before Oct 2026 | Same |
| Base of % | Possibly gross | Net Proceeds (after preferred + 5% top-up) | Same, minus delayed/contingent payments |
| Enforceability | Contract right (WI law) | Board sole discretion | Board discretion, but no cuts to shares/formula without consent |
| Condition | None | Broad release within 60 days | Same |

The restatement closes most of the gap on forfeiture — the biggest downgrade in the original trade.

- **New since the restatement:** the "leave" rows are real. Leaving at ~Apr 2027 locks ~50%, payable if MM sells *at any later date* (and Juan stays clear of Cause and covenant breaches until then). Under the August version, leaving meant $0.
- **At $1B, subordination barely matters** — it's a *small-exit* problem; it guts modest exits (or zeros a sale below the preference stack) but not this one.
- Everything scales **linearly** with the true fraction — if the cap table says 0.15% or 0.05%, adjust ±50%. Later dilution lowers all of these.

## Exit odds — the Gruns fact-check (2026-07-27)
Juan's bull case rested on: *"Mars Men has the same investor (L Catterton) as Gruns, which sold fast."* **Verified via web search — the premise is false.**
- **TRUE:** Gruns sold to **Unilever for ~$1.2B**, closed **June 1, 2026**, ~30 months after founding (Aug 2023); hit $300M+ revenue in ~2 years. A real, huge, fast exit.
- **FALSE:** **Gruns was NOT an L Catterton company.** Series B ($35M, $500M valuation, Apr 2025) was **led by Headline**; other investors Sugar Capital, Selva Ventures, Able Partners, Plus Capital, GRT SHT Ventures, and Unilever (strategic). No L Catterton in any source.
- **Lesson:** the fast exit was driven by **explosive sustained growth + a hot category + a strategic buyer (Unilever)** — not the financial sponsor. The right comp axis for Mars Men is **growth trajectory, not shared investor.** On growth (0→$100M run rate in <18 months), MM genuinely rhymes with Gruns — so a fast exit isn't fantasy, but the bull case rests on **MM sustaining growth + a strategic buyer appearing**, which nobody can predict or control.
- **Base rate:** most PE-backed consumer brands that exit at all take **5–8+ years** from investment; fast exits are the exception.

## Decision synthesis
1. **Sign the restated award.** Juan gains far more (no post-sale service rule; vested shares survive leaving; no cuts without consent) than he gives up (Cause/covenant forfeiture of vested shares).
2. **It's still not a reason to stay.** Stay/leave decides on the [[strategy]] gates: $25K+/mo recurring ASTEROI with clients Tincho can service; a live inbound engine; 3+ months runway. Plan exits ~Q2 2027; ≤2yr is the backstop. **Don't leave early off-plan** — the MM salary funds the runway and the credential powers the inbound.
3. **What did change: it's now a ticket Juan keeps after leaving.** Before, the only collection path was an early sale while still engaged. Now every month past Oct 20, 2026 adds value that survives the exit. That's a mild argument for not leaving *just before* a vesting month, not an argument to stay longer.
4. **The award now shapes ASTEROI's client choices after Juan leaves.** Until a sale (or until the 24-month post-term covenant expires), a competing men's-health client could forfeit the vested shares. Weigh any such client against the ~$400K+ ticket.
5. **Don't let equity feelings drive the stay decision either way** — Juan was never staying for upside (salary + credential + learning, all intact).
