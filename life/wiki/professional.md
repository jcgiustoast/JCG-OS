## Contract & Constraints

### Non-compete / confidentiality (ACTUAL contract, Section 5 — read 2026-07-20)
The vault previously recorded this as "US subscription brands only." **That is wrong for the during-term case.** The real structure has two layers:

- **DURING the engagement (§5.2) — WORLDWIDE, no geographic limit.** Contractor shall not "engage in any activity competitive with or adverse to the business, practice or affairs of Mars Men." No "US," no "Restricted Territory," no "subscription." While Juan holds the contract, *any* competitive-or-adverse activity is barred anywhere on earth. Reinforced by warranty §2.1(e) (won't accept work "inconsistent or incompatible" with his obligations).
- **Confidentiality (§5.6) — geography-blind, Term + 2 years.** Can't use/disclose Mars Men Confidential Information / know-how "in any geographic location where [it] could be used in competition." The Mars Men **playbook itself** is Confidential Information — so "different market" is no defense for the one asset Juan would actually bring to a competitor.
- **POST-term covenants (§5.3, 5.4, 5.8) — US-limited.** Tied to **Restricted Territory = US counties** where MM operates/plans, and to Restricted Clients/Prospects. **Restricted Period = 24 months** after the term ends. *This* is the layer the old "US only" note described — true only after Juan leaves MM.
- **"Mars Men's Business" is defined very broadly** — a "DTC brand holding company... across diverse industries" — which makes the §5.2 "competitive or adverse" net wide.
- **Governing law: Wisconsin, exclusive WI courts; loser-pays if MM prevails (§5.9, §7.4); injunctive relief available.** (Note: WI courts void, rather than narrow, overbroad covenants — a *defense if sued*, not a green light.)
- **Real risk axis = product/demographic overlap, NOT US-vs-international.** Current ASTEROI clients (Australian nail dip, Spanish/Australian non-hormone supplements, German helmets, teen grooming) are clean under any reasonable read. A same-demographic men's hormone-wellness brand is the outlier — see the **Jello** decision (declined 2026-07-20) in [[projects]].
- **§7.2:** no subcontracting/assignment of Juan's MM services without written consent.

- **Equity:** **0.1% phantom, payable only on a company sale** (change-of-control payout, not a standing stake) — retention package after Raheel left; MM never improved it, Juan stayed on the ASTEROI-bridge logic, not the number. See [[pet-brand-offer]].
- **Exit timeline:** Planning to leave in ~12 months (~Q1-Q2 2027), once ASTEROI reaches $25-30K/month gross and Tincho can service clients independently.
- **Content constraint:** Juan can freely publish about CRO, experimentation, subscription economics, retention/LTV, and eComm finance — these are his actual domain at Mars Men. He CANNOT publicly discuss Meta Ads strategy or performance marketing tactics — not his lane, and it would look strange to Zach/Benjamin since Juan doesn't touch paid acquisition at Mars Men.
- **Side work awareness:** Mars Men is not aware of ASTEROI, the agency clients, or AURAL.
