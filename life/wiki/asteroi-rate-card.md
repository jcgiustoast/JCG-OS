# ASTEROI Rate Card & Prospect-Audit Template

> The canonical pricing the next prospect audit pulls from. Don't rebuild from scratch — swap in the prospect's personas/angles and keep this skeleton. First worked example: **Manly Landing Page & CRO Audit** (Notion).

## The ladder (ascending — each tier is built to climb)

| Rung | Tier | Price / mo | 3-mo engagement |
|------|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Entry** | Advisory — Weekly Strategy | $2,000 | $6,000 |
| **Wedge** | Landing Pages — Brief only | $4,000 ($1,000 × 4) | $12,000 |
| **Wedge** | Landing Pages — Design + Dev | $4,500 ($1,500 × 3) | $13,500 |
| **Engine** | CRO — Consultant | $4,500 | $13,500 |
| **Engine** | CRO — Design + Dev | $6,500 | $19,500 |
| **Anchor** | Bundle — Growth Program | $10,000 | $30,000 |

All USD. **3-month minimum on every tier.** Experimentation needs time to reach significance and compound; the minimum also protects revenue and filters tire-kickers.

## Tier detail

### Advisory — Weekly Strategy ($2,000/mo)
Standing **1-hour strategy call each week**, plus a **dedicated Slack channel** for questions between calls. Direct access to set priorities, pressure-test plans, unblock decisions. **Advisory only — no deliverables, builds, or analysis docs** (those ascend to the tracks below). **Limited slots.**
- ~$500/hr effective. Sits cleanly below the $4,500 Consultant tier so it doesn't cannibalize.
- This is the one tier that sells Juan's *calendar* — non-scalable, Juan-only (the value is the $100M-brand brain, not Tincho's). It earns its slot only as a **wedge that ascends** or a **scarce premium**, never a comfortable terminal product.
- Flex: down to $1,500 (pure frictionless wedge) / up to $2,500 (scarce premium, protects the CRO tier from downsell).

### Landing Pages
The fast, high-velocity testing surface. The **wedge** — cheap, visible, fast.
- **Brief only — $1,000/page, min 4/mo ($4,000):** conversion strategy, structure, copy direction. Client's team builds. For prospects with in-house design/dev.
- **Design + Dev — $1,500/page, min 3/mo ($4,500):** the brief, fully designed and built test-ready. **1 revision round included** (cap revisions — the build margin is baked into the $1,500 and scope creep eats it).

### CRO & Experimentation
The structured program that compounds. The **engine** — recurring, Tincho-operated, the thing the case-study proof (`$543K/mo validated`) is about. **Up to 4 experiments/mo; a complex experiment counts as 2.**
- **Consultant — $4,500/mo:** hypotheses, test design, prioritized backlog, analysis. Client builds the experiments.
- **Design + Dev — $6,500/mo:** full cycle — designed, built, QA'd, launched, read out, documented.

### Bundle — Growth Program ($10,000/mo)
LP (Design + Dev) **+** CRO (Design + Dev) run as one program. $11,000 à la carte → **$10,000** as a light incentive to commit. This is how the $4K wedge becomes a $10K anchor.

## Paid Media (high-spend clients — performance-based)

A different model from the productized tiers above. For brands spending enough that managing the media *is* the engagement (~$50K/mo+ ad spend). **Tincho-operated, Juan behind the curtain** — paid media is Juan's Mars Men off-limits zone, so Tincho must front *and* run it.

**Price: $5,000/mo base + 10% of paid-channel contribution margin** (revenue - COGS - variable costs - ad spend). 3-month minimum.

Design rules (or it under-pays / becomes a monthly fight):
- **Base is only ~5% of spend at $100K/mo** — the CM share carries the rest to reach a fair 10-15% all-in. Calibrate the % to their margins; if paid runs thin (~2x ROAS), raise the base or the %.
- **Define CM in the contract** (rev - COGS - ad spend - shipping - fees - returns) and require **P&L access** — no data, no deal (also a good qualifier).
- **Base must clear Tincho's cost-to-serve** — it's all you get in a flat month.
- **Total vs incremental CM:** total at ~10% reaches fair comp with a low base; incremental is fairer to the client but needs a much higher %. First deal → total CM + low base.
- **Sell the full funnel:** Paid Media + angle-matched LPs (the LP tiers above), never media buying alone — that's what separates ASTEROI from a commodity buyer.

Take at $100K/mo spend (30% COGS, 12% other variable, CM after ad spend, 10% of CM):

| Paid ROAS | Contribution margin | Your take/mo | % of spend |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.0x | $16K | $6,600 | 6.6% |
| 2.5x | $45K | $9,500 | 9.5% |
| 3.0x | $74K | $12,400 | 12.4% |
| 3.5x | $103K | $15,300 | 15.3% |

Self-calibrating: market rate when the account is healthy, base protects you when it's not. First applied: **VAHDAM** (~$100K/mo).

## Delivery model (who does what)

| Tier | Delivered by | Scales? |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Advisory | **Juan only** (capped slots) | No — sells scarce calendar |
| LP — Brief | Juan (strategy/brief) | Limited — Juan-bound |
| LP — Design + Dev | Juan + tooling (LP Generator) | Partly |
| CRO — Consultant | Juan strategy, client builds | Partly |
| CRO — Design + Dev | **Juan strategy + Tincho operates** | **Yes — the engine** |

CRO is the only line Tincho can *front* — which is what lets ASTEROI scale on the Mars Men firewall while Juan stays strategic-and-stealth (see [[strategy]] Phase 1). Price CRO to be picked, not to be precious. **Open dependency:** Tincho isn't fully off 18PM until ~Sept 2026 — don't sell a Tincho-operated program before he can run it (stage the start or cover month 1, which burns the leverage).

## On-ramp (how we start)
1. **Free pitch audit** — diagnosis + sizing. Themes and a couple of teaser experiments. Sells the program; gives away nothing executable.
2. **Retainer** — pick a track above. 3-month minimum. **The full prioritized, sized experiment backlog is built in month 1**, not handed over free during the sale.

> A $2,000 flat "Metric Tree teardown" on-ramp was considered and **dropped** (Juan didn't like the deliverable-invoice feel). If a prospect balks at 3 months sight-unseen, reintroduce a paid on-ramp in different clothes — a **2-week paid sprint** or **month 1 at a trial rate** — rather than a separate teardown fee.

## Pricing principles (the logic, so the numbers survive future edits)
1. **Price on value + positioning, not hours.** The credential ("Head of eComm at a brand that did 0→$100M, L Catterton-backed") + proof (`cases.asteroi.co`) puts ASTEROI in the specialist band, not the freelancer band. Tooling makes the build cheap — don't price that saving away.
2. **The brief is the moat; the build is the commodity.** Consultant/brief tiers run **~⅔ of the full-build price** — the thinking is most of the value, execution adds less.
3. **3-month minimum, everything.** Compounding takes time; protects revenue; filters tire-kickers.
4. **Advisory is capped + scoped.** It sells Juan's scarcest, least-scalable asset. Wedge or premium, never terminal. Scope wall: the call + light async are in; deliverables are out.
5. **The ladder must ascend and not cannibalize.** Advisory < CRO Consultant on price; scope walls keep deliverables in the paid tracks; every tier is designed to climb to the next.
6. **Sell outcomes, not units.** Pair LP/CRO output with CVR / new-spend-unlocked / validated-revenue reporting. Justifies price, makes the work un-fireable, creates the expansion path.
7. **Right-size by tier and volume, not by discounting the rate.** One price list for everyone — a small client buys *less* (Advisory / fewer LPs), a big client buys *more* (Bundle / Paid Media). Cut the per-unit rate and the day a big client learns the small one's price, your anchor is gone.
8. **Performance fees attach to spend ASTEROI demonstrably influences — never to noisy attribution.** Two patterns, both keyed to ad spend, never to per-test "validated revenue" (that was considered and dropped: too noisy to attribute, a monthly fight at invoice time):
   - **Paid Media** (ASTEROI runs the account and owns the spend lever): base + **% of paid-channel contribution margin**, CM defined in-contract (rev − COGS − variable), P&L/data access required. First applied: VAHDAM.
   - **LP-launch** (ASTEROI builds the pages paid traffic lands on but does not run the media): **% of ad spend routed to each ASTEROI-built LP for its first 30 days live** — the fresh-attribution window, where the page is purely ASTEROI's work before fatigue, client edits, or season muddy it — then it rolls off. Measured on spend-by-URL from the client's existing dashboard: no P&L, no holdout, no per-test attribution. Self-capping (30-day window per page) and decoupled from who buys the media. First applied: Manly (1%).
   CRO experimentation itself has no clean spend lever, so it **stays a flat retainer** — no performance layer.

## Prospect-audit template (the shape that sells)
- **Diagnosis, not prescription.** Free audit shows *where* the money leaks + rough sizing. The *how* (ranked, designed backlog) lives behind the retainer paywall.
- **Structure:** Metric Tree (locate + size the leak) → strategic themes (each a falsifiable hypothesis, not a hunch) → 1–2 teaser experiments per theme → "full backlog is built once we start."
- **Don't list 10 ideas.** Show 2, say you have 10, sell the prioritization + execution. The count isn't the value.
- **Worked example:** Manly audit (Notion) — personas (teen / dad / mom), angles (Evil Big Soap, hormone disruption…), themes incl. **Trust & Safety** (parent-buying-for-teen → PDP must answer the safety fear the ads create).
