# MentorPass — Juan listed as mentor

## Status

- Juan is on the **mentor side** (people book and pay for time with him), not the member side.

- Details still to confirm — see [Open fields](#open-fields).

## Why this matters (strategic read)

**1. It lands exactly on the current binding constraint.**
As of 2026-07-20 the constraint moved from **capacity → demand** ([[projects]]): Tincho is operational ~1 month early (Milestone A1 done), VAHDAM is cold, Jello declined, and there is **no repeatable acquisition engine yet** (Track B). MentorPass is a pipeline asset arriving the same month the bottleneck became pipeline. That timing is the whole point of this page.

**2. It is the one growth channel that is capacity-safe.**
[[projects]] records Juan at **maximum personal capacity — cannot take one more client**. Mentor calls are 30-60 minutes with **no delivery, no retainer, no account**. It monetizes and markets his expertise without adding servicing load. This is why it fits where another client would not.

**3. It is a proven-shape channel, upgraded.**
GLAMRDiP ($4.5K/mo, the highest-margin engagement since it's direct, not through ASTEROI) came from **The Starters** — a freelance platform. Same mechanic: **prospects find Juan through the platform, not through his LinkedIn**, so nothing ties a Mars Men employee to an agency pitch. Risk level: **low**, same row as "The Starters" in the stealth-acquisition table.

**4. It compounds the personal brand.**
Phase 1's stated objective is credibility as "Head of eComm at a 9-figure L Catterton-backed brand" ([[strategy]], [[content-strategy]]). A vetted-mentor listing is third-party validation of exactly that claim, and it's an asset that keeps working after he leaves MM.

## Rules & constraints

### The sharp risk: §5.6 confidentiality (not §5.2)
Mentorship is literally *"tell me what's working at your brand."* It is **synchronous, unscripted, and the member is paying precisely to extract specifics** — a far leakier surface than a written LinkedIn post Juan can edit before publishing.

Per [[professional]], the **Mars Men playbook itself is Confidential Information** under **§5.6, which is geography-blind and runs Term + 2 years**. So the operative rule is not "don't name Mars Men" — it's **don't hand over the MM playbook**, even anonymized, even to a non-competitor.

**Working rule:** teach the *method* (how to structure a test, how to think about subscription LTV, how to read a CRO result), never the *proprietary specifics* (MM's actual test results, funnel math, LP performance, retention numbers, roadmap). Method is portable and it's what mentees are paying for anyway. Specifics are the contract breach.

### §5.2 — who is allowed to book him
**§5.2 bars any activity "competitive with or adverse to" Mars Men, WORLDWIDE, during the term.** On a marketplace, Juan does not choose who books — bookings come to him. That inverts the usual vetting.

**Decline rule:** if the booker is a **men's hormone / testosterone / male-wellness-supplement brand**, decline and refund. This is the **Jello** line ([[projects]] → Declined/blocked) — product/demographic overlap is the real risk axis, not geography. Everything else (nail care, helmets, teen grooming, non-hormone supplements) is clean under any reasonable read.
- **Action needed:** confirm MentorPass lets a mentor decline/cancel a booking without penalty. If it does not, that is a structural problem with being listed at all.

### Content constraint carries over
Juan **cannot discuss Meta Ads / performance marketing** ([[professional]]) — not his lane at MM and it would read strangely to Zach/Benjamin. Sellable topics are the ones he actually owns: **CRO & experimentation, subscription economics, retention/LTV, eComm finance, LP design/dev.** If someone books him for paid media: decline, or hand to Tincho.

### Firewall — the direction that matters
The usual rule is "never name Mars Men in an ASTEROI context." **MentorPass runs the other way.** It's a personal-brand surface, so leading with the MM credential is consistent with his LinkedIn and fine.

The risk is the **reverse**: the profile is public and indexed, and a Mars Men colleague could find it. So **the profile must not position Juan as an agency founder** — no "Founder, ASTEROI," no agency services, no client roster. MentorPass is the one place where "MM Head of eComm" and "agency owner" would appear in the same frame. Mentor = thought leader (safe). Mentor + agency = the firewall breach.

### Routing converted leads
A mentee who wants ongoing work must route to **Tincho / ASTEROI**, never to Juan personally — he has no capacity.
- **Verify first:** most marketplaces bar taking a relationship **off-platform**. Check MentorPass's terms before converting any mentee into an ASTEROI retainer. If circumvention is barred, MentorPass is a **brand/credibility channel that happens to pay**, not a client-acquisition funnel — which materially changes how much it's worth investing in.
