## Decision Frame — ASTEROI Bridge (2026-06-01, latest)

**What actually happened:** Mars Men countered — **cash matched** ($230K base + $50K bonus) but only **0.1% equity** (vs the 0.3–0.75% Raheel intel implied). Lowball, or an opening bid.

**Juan's correction (key insight):** PAK selling for $250M off ~$15M revenue is a long-shot (~16x; DTC pet trades 2–5x). Realistic PAK outcome: 2–3x growth to ~$30–45M ARR, exit ~$90–135M at 3x → **time slug only, ~$210–340K**, four years out. The $1.18M (at $250M) is a lottery ticket; $3.6M (at $500M) is fantasy.

**So the equity is ~a wash:**

| | Realistic equity | Horizon | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mars Men 0.1% | ~$300–500K | ~2 yr | High (PE sell-mandate, already at scale) |
| PAK 0.75% | ~$210–340K | ~4 yr | Lower (must 5x+ a $15M brand) |

Cash tied, equity roughly tied → **comp no longer decides.**

**The deciding frame: ASTEROI is the wealth engine; the job is a 2-year cash + credential BRIDGE.** Optimize the bridge for (a) lowest cost to cross and (b) highest certainty it lands. Mars Men wins both:
- **Closer + surer exit** — sooner liquidity (~2 yr), less execution risk, lined up with the ASTEROI go-full-time window.
- **Near-zero disruption** — no relocation, no new-job ramp during the Phase 1 stealth build. The real advantage is **bandwidth + an already-built firewall**: at MM Juan has slack and ASTEROI is hidden; PAK's first year consumes more and (new contract, reporting to Raheel) risks more visibility.
- **Better single credential** — "took a brand to a $300–500M exit, L Catterton-backed" beats a 12-month pet-brand stint for the US-DTC personal-brand lane. Leaving mid-build is a credential downgrade.

**Consequence — the equity fight shrinks.** Because MM's value is a calm, hidden, 2-year runway + clean credential, Juan needs the *relationship* intact more than an extra 0.2%. So: ask cleanly, take what they give (even if modest), **don't torch the relationship.** Equity is upside on the bridge, not the point.

**Equity-timing reality:** Don't count on a future raise bringing more equity. Near an exit the equity window *closes* (value concrete, cap table cleaned for the sale, no retention runway). The number negotiated now is likely the **final exit stake.** Peak leverage is now.

### Current recommendation
**Lean STAY at Mars Men, run it as the ASTEROI bridge** — even at ~0.1% — *provided* the 2027 exit is credible (L Catterton-aligned, not founder hope). Make the clean equity counter (anchor ~0.5% on internal fairness; settle for whatever; don't fight to the death).

**Flip to PAK only if:**
- Gut says **Raheel is a genuine career-multiplier** worth the disruption (the one variable the math can't price), OR
- The **MM exit timeline is hazy** (thin equity + uncertain exit → PAK's fresh start + Raheel wins).

**Two self-checks before committing to stay:**
1. **Don't coast.** The bridge only works if the protected bandwidth actually goes into ASTEROI (content, Tincho, SOPs). Stay = deadline-driven bridge, not a hammock.
2. **Motive test:** if PAK required *zero* disruption (same city, no ramp), would Raheel + bigger scope still pull you? If yes → "protects ASTEROI" is partly a comfort story; weight Raheel heavier. If no → the stay choice is genuinely strategic.
